FAQ

Questions, answered.

Everything you might want to know before you start. Can't find your answer? Sign up and ask the AI coach.

FocusForge is one place to plan, focus and improve. It brings together tasks with due times, habits, goals with milestones, a Pomodoro focus timer and study sessions, with an AI coach that knows your work. Everything you finish earns XP, so progress feels rewarding.

Yes. FocusForge runs in your browser on any phone, tablet or laptop, so there's nothing to install. Your data is saved to your account, so you can start on one device and pick up on another.

Tasks with priorities, categories, tags and due times. Habits every day or on the weekdays you choose, with streaks and a 30-day completion rate. Goals broken into milestones, focus sessions from 5 to 120 minutes, and study sessions per subject with your files attached.

You earn +10 XP for a task, +5 for a habit check-in, +15 for a focus session, +20 for a milestone and +50 for finishing a goal. Every 100 XP takes you to the next level, and there are 52 achievements to unlock along the way.

It reads your own tasks, habits and goals, then tells you what to do first, plans your day or evening and reviews how your habits are going. It can answer as a clear table and suggest tasks, habits or goals to add.

Yes. You can download weekly and monthly PDF reports with your focus time, finished tasks, habit check-ins, goal progress and highlights. They're a quick way to see how your week or month really went.

Yes. Your tasks, habits, goals, study files and AI chats are visible only to you. Even the admin panel shows just basic account details and activity counts, never your content. You can export your data or delete your account at any time from Settings.
